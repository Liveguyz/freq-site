Contact point under the Digital Services Act

The single contact point under Art. 11 DSA (authorities, Commission, Board) and Art. 12 DSA (users) is the e-mail address above. Languages: German and English. You can report content in the app ("Report") or by e-mail to the same address; decisions are given with reasons and can be appealed by e-mail. The supervisory authority in Austria is KommAustria.

Content and purpose

FREQ is a non-commercial development project by a private individual: a convoy app for driving together with friends. This website provides information about the app and hosts the legal documents.

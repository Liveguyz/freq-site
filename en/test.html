Test FREQ

The test version comes straight from the Play Store and also runs in Android Auto. Android only. Two steps and you're in.

  1. Join the test group

    Open the group with the Google account you use in the Play Store and tap “Join group”.

    Open the test group

  2. Install FREQ

    Tap “Become a tester”, then “Download it on Google Play”. If it still says “not available”, wait a few minutes after step 1 and reload the page.

    Become a tester

Got FREQ through WhatsApp already? Uninstall that app first, otherwise the Play Store can't install FREQ. Your account stays – just sign in again afterwards.

Android Auto

As soon as your phone is connected to the car, FREQ shows up in Android Auto by itself. You don't need the “Unknown sources” setting for it.

What the test version sends

The test version sends technical data for finding bugs – for example crashes, battery and heat, the route you drove and your search terms. That is the condition for taking part: on first start FREQ shows exactly what is sent, and you agree. Nothing is listened to. If you don't want that, don't take part in the test. More in the privacy policy.

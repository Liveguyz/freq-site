Drive together.
Talk together.

FREQ is the convoy app for you and your friends — navigation, radio, and one shared destination, routed individually on every phone.

FREQ is under development and not yet available on the App Store or Google Play. This page is the official home for privacy, terms, and support.

Radio like a walkie-talkie

Press the key and speak — everyone in the convoy hears you. Quiet mode, wake-up call, and calling included.

One destination for everyone

The admin sets the place; every phone navigates on its own, with its own route from where it is.

Arrive relaxed

Voice guidance, speed, and speed-camera warnings where they are legal. Community meets and spots built in.

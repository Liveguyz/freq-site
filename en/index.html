Convoy: everyone on one map.

Everyone rides along as a face on your map. The admin sets the destination, every phone computes its own route – and everyone arrives at the same spot.

For example: Anna sets the destination, Ben leaves ten minutes later – his route starts right where he is.

Radio: talk like you’re in the same car.

An open channel for everyone in the convoy – no button to press. “Quiet” when you want music, “Call Anna” when you need someone. Nothing is recorded.

Warned before it gets tight.

Two warnings instead of constant beeping – for fixed speed cameras in more than 30 countries, only where it is legal. The speed limit sits next to your speed. And when someone in the convoy brakes hard, everyone sees it.

For example: “70 in 400 m” announces the new limit, then FREQ warns of the camera – and the whole convoy eases off.

Navigation with its own voice.

Clear announcements, for example “In 300 metres, turn right” – with lane arrows and up to three routes to choose from. Avoid motorways or tolls if you like.

Average speed zones: your average, live.

Inside the zone FREQ shows your average next to the limit – on the car display also what is left: “2.4 km left”. Austria’s zones come from the official regulations.

Fuel, break, food – for everyone.

In a convoy the admin sets the stop for everyone. Fuel stations show the price in Austria, charging stations show free or occupied in the first countries. After two hours FREQ quietly suggests a break.

In the car, on the big screen.

Built for CarPlay and Android Auto: speedometer with speed limit, camera panel with distance, your convoy as faces, radio on screen – part of FREQ Premium.

Car meets near you.

Meets from scene calendars, right inside FREQ: one tap sets the route, and you see how many are on their way. Not everywhere yet.

Free to start.
No ads.

Navigation, warnings and reporting stay free. FREQ Premium adds the car display and large convoys: €2.99 a month or €24.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial. Not in the stores yet – built in Vienna.

Credits for the 3D world

Everything on the start page comes from free sources or was built for FREQ. As of 23 November 2026.

3D models

All models were adapted for the website (simplified, new materials and lights, brands removed). Licence CC BY 4.0 in each case.

  • “Stylish street light pole with fixtures duo” by AsXSn — source — modified (street lights)
  • “EV_charging_model” by yuki — source — modified (charging stations)
  • “Small Red Gas Station” by SmashedBones — source — modified (fuel station, no brand)
  • “Autonomous GT Car Interior Design – Manual Mode” by benlockett — source — modified (interior)

The vehicles at the car meet and the motorhome at the rest area (the same as in the app), the saloon car, the conifers, the interior, fuel pumps, speed camera, billboard and signs of the first version were built for FREQ (Blender), as were all profile pictures (drawn, no real people).

Software

  • three.js (with GLTFLoader, HDRLoader and the meshopt decoder) — MIT licence, text in lizenzen/three.js-MIT.txt
  • Models prepared with Blender and glTF Transform
  • Maps in the app: data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); the map on the in-car screen is a schematic imitation
  • Speed cameras in the app (supplement, Austria): MapAtlas Camera Index by MapAtlas, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Modified: only places without a known camera, without average speed sections and direction. Average speed sections in Austria: official ordinances from the RIS.
  • Speed limits in Austria: data source gip.gv.at (Graph Integration Platform GIP.at), licensed under CC BY 4.0. Modified: where OpenStreetMap has no value, FREQ adds speed limits from GIP – only those that the GIP data itself confirms as a posted sign – and stores them in its own map files. All other speed limits come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL).
  • Speed cameras in Tyrol (motorways): information provided by the Office of the Tyrolean Provincial Government under the Austrian Freedom of Information Act, 11 November 2026.
  • Speed cameras in the districts of Schwaz and Landeck: information from the municipality of Schwaz (via the district authority Schwaz), the district authority Schwaz and the district authority Landeck, 12 Nov 2026. Location: OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) and, on provincial roads, the kilometre reference points from GIP – data source gip.gv.at, licence CC BY 4.0.
  • Red-light enforcement at level crossings of the Zillertal Railway: information from Zillertaler Verkehrsbetriebe AG via the district authority Schwaz, 12 Nov 2026. Location: OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL).

The full list with all changes is in the repository under lizenzen/ASSETS.md.

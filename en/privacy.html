Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · applies to the FREQ app (iOS and Android) and this website.

Who is responsible

Controller under the GDPR: Luka Veselinovic, Nordwestbahnstraße 91/39, 1200 Vienna, Austria — lukaves567@gmail.com.

The short version

  • FREQ does not sell data and shows no ads.
  • Your dot on the map is shown only to the members of your running convoy. Beyond that, your location goes to map, search and price services when you use them — and with "friends nearby" as well as speed-camera and hazard reports, an entry sits in the database that other drivers can read. The details are in the table below.
  • The radio is an open channel, not a push-to-talk key. Once you are on a convoy's radio, your microphone is open and the others hear you — even with the screen off. "Quiet" or muting ends that immediately. Outside a convoy the microphone is off.
  • Conversations are never recorded or stored — they pass live through the voice server and are gone afterwards.
  • The app never listens secretly and does not analyse anything you say.

Recipients

Depending on use, location, destination or IP data also reach: Photon/komoot (address search), OSRM (routing), Overpass (fuel, parking, food), E-Control (Austrian fuel prices), Open Charge Map (charging points), and GitHub, Inc. as the host of this website. The full list with licences is in the app under Settings → Privacy → Sources and licences.

Transfers outside the EU

Part of the processing happens outside the EU:

  • USA: Google (Firebase Authentication, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging), Cloudflare, GitHub. The basis is the adequacy decision on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795 of 10 July 2023); EU standard contractual clauses under Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR apply in addition.
  • United Kingdom: OpenStreetMap Foundation. The basis is the European Commission's adequacy decision for the United Kingdom.

A copy of the standard contractual clauses is available on request at the e-mail address above.

Legal bases

Processing is based on the user agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — sign-in, convoy, radio, navigation), on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) — abuse prevention and operational security), and, where the app explicitly asks you, on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a); for crash reports additionally § 165(3) Austrian TKG 2021): location, microphone, crash reports and speed-camera warnings in countries requiring opt-in. You can withdraw any consent at any time in the settings.

Reports and automatic suspension

If three different accounts report the same account within 30 days, it is suspended automatically and provisionally — without prior human review, for at most 7 days. You see the reason in the app and can object by e-mail at any time; the case is then reviewed by a human. Stored for this are the reporter's identifier, the reported account, the reason and the time.

Retention

A convoy's position data is deleted when the convoy ends; a server job cleans up orphaned convoy rooms. Your account and friends list remain until you delete your account (in the app settings or by e-mail). Crashlytics retains crash reports for 90 days.

Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and the right to complain to the Austrian data protection authority (dsb.gv.at). Contact the e-mail address above for any of these.

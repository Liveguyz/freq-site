Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · applies to the FREQ app (iOS and Android) and this website.

Who is responsible

The short version

  • FREQ does not sell data and shows no ads.
  • Your dot on the map is shown only to the members of your running convoy. Beyond that, your location goes to map, search, routing and price services when you use them, and rounded to our own services for weather, places and charging points — and with "friends nearby" as well as speed-camera and hazard reports, an entry sits in the database that other drivers can read. The details are in the table below.
  • Background location: As long as you are signed in, FREQ keeps locating you while the screen is off or you use another app — so that speed-camera alerts and navigation keep running. Your location is evaluated on your device; it only reaches our servers in a convoy, when you search for "friends nearby" and for routes (details in the table and under "Recipients"). On Android a permanent notification "FREQ is active" shows this. It ends when you sign out or close FREQ.
  • In a convoy you also share your battery level (the others see a sign when it is nearly empty — you can turn this off) and your hard braking: if you brake hard from 60 km/h or more, everyone on your convoy's radio gets the notice "Hard braking in the convoy" — without your name and without your location (you can turn this off). The battery level ends with the convoy; hard-braking events are not stored at all.
  • The radio is an open channel, not a push-to-talk key. Once you are on a convoy's radio, your microphone is open and the others hear you — even with the screen off. "Quiet" or muting ends that immediately. Outside a convoy the microphone is off.
  • Conversations are never recorded or stored — they pass live through the voice server and are gone afterwards.
  • The app never listens in secretly. So that you can call hands-free in a convoy ("Call Anna"), FREQ recognises on your phone, while the radio is on, whether you say a name or a command such as "Quiet". This recognition runs only on your device: on iPhone with Apple's on-device speech recognition, on Android (version 13 or newer) with Android's on-device speech recognition. Neither your audio nor the recognised text is stored or sent to us or anyone else. Only what you trigger, such as a call to a convoy member, goes to the convoy. You can turn this off in Settings under "Radio & microphone" with "Call by voice".

Recipients

Depending on use, location, destination or IP data also reach:

  • Photon/komoot (Germany): address search, with your current location for nearby results; place names in the trip log.
  • Nominatim/OpenStreetMap Foundation (United Kingdom): turning an address into coordinates.
  • Routing servers: for routes within Austria up to 150 km first our own server at Hetzner in Germany, otherwise and as a fallback the public servers routing.openstreetmap.de (FOSSGIS e. V.) and router.project-osrm.org. They receive your location or the start, your heading, a waypoint and the destination.
  • Overpass (servers of the OpenStreetMap community): fixed speed cameras and, as a fallback, fuel, parking, food and car washes (location rounded to about 100 m); points of your route for the speed limit and its preview.
  • Fuel prices: E-Control (Vienna), Tankerkönig.de (Germany), Ministère de l'Économie via data.economie.gouv.fr (France) and Ministerio para la Transición Ecológica y el Reto Demográfico (MITECO, Spain).
  • Open Charge Map: charging stations.
  • OpenFreeMap: map tiles, also for offline maps.
  • Our own server at Hetzner (Hetzner Online GmbH, Gunzenhausen, Germany): voice server (radio and hard-braking notice), routes in Austria and "avoid motorways", places, closures, entrances and rest areas, live charging points; it also collects the community meets and counts the "going" entries.
  • Our service at Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA): radio ticket, notifications, reports, "friends nearby", account deletion, and weather and weather warnings.
  • GitHub, Inc. as the host of this website.

No data about you reaches the bodies whose data FREQ only obtains through our own servers: the official weather services GeoSphere Austria (Vienna), Deutscher Wetterdienst (Offenbach am Main, Germany), EUMETNET – MeteoAlarm (managed by GeoSphere Austria) and MET Norway (Oslo, Norway) — they only receive requests from our service with rounded points not linked to you; the charging data portals; treffeninfo.de and carnation.at. foto-webcam.eu receives nothing from FREQ either; only when you open a camera page there in your browser does the operator's privacy policy apply. The full list of sources with licences is in the app under Settings → Privacy & legal → Sources and licences, and in the Imprint.

What stays on your device only

FREQ keeps the following only in the app storage of your device; we have no access to it:

  • your profile (radio name, picture, vehicle icon, identifier) and your settings — except the map view, which is stored with your account (see the table),
  • home, work, favourites and recent destinations,
  • learned routes (start, destination, route, average duration, number of trips) — "Learn routes" is on by default and can be switched off under Settings → Privacy & legal,
  • the trip log (see the table), including the backup of the trip in progress,
  • the route you last drove (destination, waypoint) and the convoy you were last in, so that FREQ can continue after a restart or crash — both for at most 30 minutes,
  • for the vignette notice, the current trip with one checkpoint (time and place) and the countries of the trip,
  • the break timer and cached places (6 hours at most),
  • speed-camera datasets and offline maps; they contain no data about you.

Legal bases

Processing is based on the user agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — sign-in, convoy, radio, navigation), on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) — abuse prevention and operational security), and, where the app explicitly asks you, on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a); for crash reports additionally § 165(3) Austrian TKG 2021): location, microphone, crash reports and speed-camera warnings in countries requiring opt-in. You can withdraw any consent at any time in the settings.

For the new features: weather and weather warnings, closures, places and fuel-station details, fuel and charging prices, offline maps, "avoid motorways", the speed-limit preview, the break suggestion, planned rides, community meets, call by voice, and the battery level and hard-braking notice in a convoy serve the performance of the user agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — they run because you use the feature; the convoy is a feature you start yourself. The app identifier sent to OpenFreeMap is based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): the usage rules of the free map service. You can object at any time (Art. 21 GDPR, e-mail above).

Reports and automatic suspension

If three different accounts report the same account within 30 days, it is suspended automatically and provisionally — without prior human review, for at most 7 days. You see the reason in the app and can object by e-mail at any time; the case is then reviewed by a human. Stored for this are the reporter's identifier, the reported account, the reason and the time.

Retention

A convoy's position data is deleted when the convoy ends; a server job cleans up orphaned convoy rooms. The app shows speed-camera reports for 3 hours; our server deletes them, together with the link to your account, after about 8 hours. Hazard reports expire without confirmation after 1 hour (police), 1.5 hours (accident and breakdown) or 2 hours (traffic jam and roadworks), with confirmations after 3, 4 or 6 hours at the latest; our server deletes them with their answers and the reporter entry about 6 hours after the report. Entries for frequent camera spots are kept for 365 days. Your account and friends list remain until you delete your account (in the app settings or by e-mail). Crashlytics retains crash reports for 90 days. Data kept only on your device stays until you delete it, delete your account or uninstall FREQ.

For the new features: FREQ deletes your battery level in a convoy as soon as you leave the convoy; our server cleans up orphaned rooms. Hard-braking notices and the route points for weather and weather warnings are not stored; the receiving phones keep the random identifier of a hard-braking notice for 60 seconds at most, only to spot duplicates. Our server deletes planned rides 24 hours after departure, with their invitations and answers; the planner can delete them earlier at any time. FREQ deletes the "going" entry for a community meet when your route ends, at the latest after one hour without a refresh.

Server logs

When our services are accessed, the providers technically process IP address, time and type of request and log this briefly — for secure operation and against abuse (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Our Cloudflare service never logs sign-in tokens or route points, only error codes; Cloudflare keeps these logs for 3 days. Firebase Authentication: a few weeks (according to Google). Our services at Hetzner (voice server, routing server, places and charging points, community meets): 7 days at most.

Signing out and deleting your account

Signing out: FREQ leaves a running convoy, ends your online status and the notifications to this device, and switches off background location. On the device FREQ deletes your profile (radio name, picture, vehicle icon, identifier), the remembered route, the convoy you were last in and the trip of the vignette notice. Trip log, learned routes, recent destinations and your other settings stay on the device; you delete them in the settings or with your account.

Deleting your account: in the app under Settings → Account → "Delete account", or by e-mail. FREQ leaves a running convoy and deletes on our servers your online status, your friends list, your block list, your inbox, your speed-camera and hazard reports, your reports about others, your notification data, your map view, your planned rides (for other people's rides, your place and your answer), reports about you and any suspension, and finally your account. Requests and invitations you just sent to others expire there by themselves and are deleted after about a day. Only once the account is deleted does FREQ delete all FREQ data on this device — including the trip log with the trip in progress, the learned routes with the recording in progress, your friends' pictures and the generated AI icons — and reset all settings to factory defaults, including your radio name and your consent to speed-camera alerts in Germany. Offline maps contain no data about you and stay until you uninstall FREQ. FREQ cannot delete what friends saved on their phones (your name, for example), nor the e-mails we received about reports.
